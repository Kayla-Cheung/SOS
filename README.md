# SOS: Student Overload Simulator

> **In-Silico Campus Testing of Academic Anxiety Dynamics and Sociological Interventions**
>
> An experimental research framework exploring Theory of Mind (ToM), emotional contagion, and behavioral intervention mechanisms in academic environments, built in synergy with [SINA](https://github.com/Kayla-Cheung/SINA) (*Sociological & Introspective Neural Architecture*).

---

## 📌 Abstract & Research Focus

Academic anxiety in collegiate ecosystems is rarely an isolated cognitive phenomenon; rather, it is a dynamic, emergent process driven by structural deadlines, peer-comparative feedback loops, and social contagion.

**SOS (Student Overload Simulator)** models:
1. **Cognitive & Affective Dynamics**: Multi-dimensional student state vectors (academic load, social support buffer, internal anxiety, burnout threshold).
2. **Theory of Mind (ToM) in Social Graphs**: Evaluating recursive perspective-taking and rumor/stress diffusion in dormitory, library, and seminar topologies.
3. **Intervention Sandbox**: Simulating and assessing the effectiveness of micro-level peer buffering, macro-level institutional pacing, and automated advisory interventions.
